## About Evermont

**Evermont Credit Union** is a modern fintech banking platform positioned as a **Secured Private Wealth Bank**.

Evermont is designed to combine the trust and member-focused principles of a credit union with the technology, convenience, security, and user experience of modern digital banking.

The platform is being developed around a professional private-wealth banking experience where members can manage financial accounts, explore banking products, monitor transactions, access lending services, manage their profiles, and interact with digital financial tools from one secure platform.

> **Project Status:** Prototype / Development  
> **Primary Frontend:** https://evermont.builder.cloud  
> **Secondary Frontend Reference:** https://v0-evermont.vercel.app

> **Important:** Evermont is currently a fictional technology prototype and is not a licensed or regulated financial institution.

---

## Vision

Evermont explores what a modern, secure, member-focused private financial institution could look like in a digital environment.

The goal is to create a professional fintech foundation that can evolve toward production when appropriate banking, regulatory, compliance, security, payment, and financial infrastructure requirements are satisfied.

## Core Features

### Member Authentication

- Member registration
- Secure login
- Session management
- Password recovery
- Protected member areas
- Supabase Authentication

### Member Dashboard

- Account overview
- Account balances
- Recent transactions
- Financial activity
- Notifications
- Member profile
- Security settings

### Banking Products

- Checking Accounts
- Savings Accounts
- Credit Cards
- 401(k)
- Personal Loans
- Auto Loans
- Crypto Loans
- Investment and wealth services

### User Profile

- Personal information
- Contact information
- Profile management
- Account settings
- Security settings
- Notifications

### Administrative Dashboard

Authorized administrators can manage and monitor:

- Members
- User profiles
- Accounts
- Transactions
- Loans
- Support requests
- Notifications
- System activity
- Administrative operations

Admin access should use proper role-based authorization and Supabase Row Level Security.

---

## AI Customer Care

Evermont is designed to include an **AI Customer Care** experience that can assist members and visitors with general questions about:

- Evermont services
- Accounts
- Banking products
- Loans
- Security
- General support

Human support requests can be routed to:

**evermontcreditunion@gmail.com**

The AI should not claim to perform financial transactions, change account balances, approve loans, or perform other regulated financial actions unless those capabilities are properly implemented and authorized on the backend.
